PROBLEM STATEMENT
02. Critical Threat: High-Energy Space Weather
⚡ D-Layer Radio Blackouts
Solar X-ray bursts instantly ionize Earth's D-layer, causing total High-Frequency (HF) radio communications blackouts for transoceanic aviation and military defense routes.
📡 NavIC / GNSS Degradation
Rapid ionospheric Total Electron Content (TEC) spikes distort satellite signals, degrading India's NavIC positioning accuracy for civil transport and defense.
🛰 Satellite SEU & Power Grids
Solar protons cause Single Event Upsets (SEUs) in satellite electronics. CME plasma shocks induce destructive Geomagnetically Induced Currents (GICs) in ground power transformers.
❌ The Legacy Bottleneck
Traditional supercomputer physics models (WSA-ENLIL) require hours to run. Standard "black-box" ML models produce unphysical, wild hallucinations during extreme X-class flares.

INNOVATION & PHYSICS
03. The Breakthrough: Physics-Informed Neural AI
🛰 Aditya-L1 Telemetry Fusion
Integrates 1s cadence raw X-ray data from ISRO's SoLEXS (1-15 keV Soft X-ray) and HEL1OS (10-150 keV Hard X-ray) payloads located at Sun-Earth L1.
🧲 MHD Loss Penalty Constraint
Embeds the Magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) induction equation ∂B/∂t = ∇×(u×B) + η∇²B directly into the ML loss function, penalizing unphysical predictions and reducing false alarms by 8.3%.
🔥 Neupert Effect Kinetics
Validates chromospheric evaporation by correlating HEL1OS hard X-rays with the time derivative of SoLEXS soft X-rays (dI/dt), capturing pre-flare thermal energy accumulation.
⏱ Sub-Second Operational Speed
Delivers instant flare nowcasts and 30-minute pre-cursor forecasts in milliseconds, giving satellite operators 15–45 minutes of advance lead-time.

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE
04. End-to-End Pipeline Architecture
1. Telemetry Ingestion
FastAPI backend processes raw photon streams from ISSDC PRADAN portal (847,752 SoLEXS & HEL1OS points). Resampled, calibrated, and time-aligned.
2. Feature Extraction
Extracts 5 key physical parameters: mean count, standard deviation, Spectral Hardness Ratio (HXR/SXR), linear derivative slope (dI/dt), and QPP oscillation power.
3. Random Forest + PINN
Classifier ensemble trained on 71,825 labeled samples (B, C, M, X flare classes) constrained by MHD loss penalty. Evaluates 10-min sliding windows.
4. Dual Control Interfaces
Outputs predictions to 3D WebGL Globe (spatial blackout maps) & 2D Streamlit Control Center (scientific analytics & RAG AI Space Assistant).
🎙️ WHAT TO SAY TO JUDGES:
"My pipeline takes raw photon telemetry from L1, extracts 5 physical features, passes them through a physics-constrained Random Forest ensemble, and outputs to both 3D and 2D mission control interfaces."

TECHNICAL Q&A SHIELD
09. Pro-Defense Against Tough Judge Questions
Q: Why Random Forest over LSTMs?
Ans: RF requires far lower latency (sub-millisecond), handles tabular telemetry features without overfitting, and provides exact Gini feature importance & SHAP explainability.
Q: How do you handle 8-min L1 light delay?
Ans: Light speed delay is an unalterable physical constant. However, our system processes data in sub-seconds upon ground arrival and provides 30-min precursor forecasts.
Q: Why represent blackouts as ellipses?
Ans: Ionospheric D-layer X-ray absorption scales primarily with solar zenith angle cos(χ), making a subsolar ellipse the optimal 1st-order physical approximation for HUD controls.
Q: What if SoLEXS payload goes offline?
Ans: The pipeline incorporates separate fallback models trained for SXR-only, HXR-only, and combined telemetry, supported by Kalman filter imputation.
